# Codex on mobile (Codex Remote)

Start, guide, and review Codex coding tasks from your phone. Follow progress, approve actions, and send instructions; Codex runs tasks in the cloud or on a connected Mac or Windows PC. Formerly referred to as Remote. Availability depends on rollout and your workspace settings.

## Signature / Usage

In the ChatGPT desktop app on the host: **Settings > Connections > Control this Mac or PC > Set up** (or **Add**), approve remote access and complete any requested verification, scan the QR code with your phone, sign in to the same ChatGPT account and workspace, approve the connection, then open **Codex** in the ChatGPT mobile app (iOS) to start or continue a task. If your mobile app still shows Remote, open that instead.

## Notes

- Source: https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/mobile (retrieved 2026-10; page title "Codex on mobile").
- Use the ChatGPT mobile app with Cloud or a connected Mac or Windows PC. Keep the host computer awake and online, signed in to the desktop app. Only connect devices you own and trust.
- Advantages: start tasks from your phone (choose Cloud and an environment, or a connected computer and project), guide work as it happens (follow progress, send new instructions), approve requested commands and actions before Codex continues on your connected computer, and review responses, changed files, diffs, and test results.
- Keep work moving from anywhere: (1) see tasks across your hosts (Cloud and connected computers, existing conversations, when your input is needed); (2) approve requests; (3) review changed code; (4) start new tasks. Codex runs the work under your organization's security policies and the connected computer's existing sandbox/approval settings.
- Explore setup and security (computer requirements, device management, permissions, troubleshooting) in [Remote connections](./remote-connections.md).
