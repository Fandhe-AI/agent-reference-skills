# Climate Commitments

Direct a fraction of your revenue to help advance carbon removal. Contributions go to projects that remove carbon dioxide (CO2) from the atmosphere so those projects can scale.

## Signature / Usage

```bash
# Set your contribution percentage (example: 1%)
stripe climate commitment enable 1

# View your current program
stripe climate commitment show

# Turn off your commitment
stripe climate commitment disable
```

## Notes

- Manage the contribution percentage and monitor impact in the Dashboard (`https://dashboard.stripe.com/climate`); Stripe also sends regular updates about your contribution.
- Climate Commitments is a revenue-percentage contribution. To make carbon neutral or net zero claims, use Climate Orders instead.
- Stripe selects projects with scientific expert reviewers and uses a transparent application process; following a portfolio approach, some projects might deliver results and others might fail.

### Connect

- Platforms can enable Climate Commitments in the Dashboard without affecting accounts on the platform.
- If you take an application fee from connected accounts, it is reflected in your percentage-based contributions.
- Destination charges: the percentage applies to the charge minus the transferred amount.
- Separate charges and transfers: the percentage applies to the entire charge amount.
- Standard accounts: new users can enable Climate contributions during onboarding via the Climate setting in the Connect Dashboard settings (`https://dashboard.stripe.com/settings/connect/climate`). Existing Standard accounts can enable it independently in their own Dashboard (doing so does not affect your platform); share `https://dashboard.stripe.com/get-started/climate` to point them to it.

### Promote your commitment

- Use the Climate badge on Stripe Checkout, Invoices, or Receipts.
- Direct users to your Stripe-hosted custom Climate webpage.
- Add an embeddable badge to your website.
- Use reference language from the Stripe Climate page.
- Custom climate page, embeddable badges, and the badge asset kit are available from the Dashboard (`https://dashboard.stripe.com/climate/commitment`).
