# Capital Customer Support (Servicing)

How to support Capital customers by region. Capital for Platforms is available in AU, CA, DE, FR, GB, and US; servicing arrangements vary by country based on the financing partner.

## Signature / Usage

Escalate every Capital-related complaint, in every region, to:

```text
capital-support@stripe.com
```

## Options / Props

| Country | First-line support | Contact | Servicing functions |
|---------|--------------------|---------|---------------------|
| US | Stripe | capital-support@stripe.com | Reviewing applications; collecting payments; maintaining records of payments and balances; general administration; responding to borrower inquiries; following up on delinquencies |
| GB | Stripe and YouLend | capital-support@stripe.com | Reviewing applications; facilitating payments; maintaining records of payments and outstanding balances; general administration; responding to borrower inquiries |
| FR | YouLend (Stripe oversight) | stripe-assistance@youlend.com | Same as GB (YouLend) |
| DE | YouLend (Stripe oversight) | stripe-assistance@youlend.com | Same as GB (YouLend) |
| AU | Stripe and Fundbox | capital-support@stripe.com | Reviewing applications; collecting payments; maintaining records; general administration; responding to borrower inquiries; following up on delinquencies |
| CA | Stripe and Fundbox | Eligibility or missed minimum payments: stripe-capital-ca@fundbox.com; all other questions: capital-support@stripe.com | Review applications; collect payments; maintain payment and balance records; administer financing; respond to borrower inquiries; follow up on delinquencies |

## Notes

- Submit any created content or material changes to approved support content through the Change Request Form (Google Doc or PDF). Stripe's product legal team, compliance team, and banking partner must approve all user-facing materials before they are sent to users.
- If your support team fields basic frontline inquiries, Stripe offers pre-approved, compliant training materials and templated responses; request them from your Stripe account team or Stripe Support.
- In the US, GB, and AU, every communication includes the support email; if a user contacts your platform about their financing, forward the user and their questions to Stripe. In FR and DE, direct connected accounts to YouLend.
- Stripe has a legal obligation to report all Capital-related complaints. Terms that can indicate a complaint include: angry, attorney, deceived, discrimination, lawsuit, litigation, mad, mislead, predatory, sue, unfair.
- Source: https://docs.stripe.com/capital/servicing
