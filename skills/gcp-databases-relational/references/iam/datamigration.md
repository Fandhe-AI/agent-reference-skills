The `service.resource.*` group headings of the official page are omitted from the per-role lists and counts, because they are collapsible headings, not permissions.
- The permissions table lists role IDs only (role names and links are on the official page) and gives service agent roles as a count; service agent roles must not be granted to principals other than service agents.
- Source: https://docs.cloud.google.com/iam/docs/roles-permissions/datamigration (CC BY 4.0; https://developers.google.com/site-policies)
