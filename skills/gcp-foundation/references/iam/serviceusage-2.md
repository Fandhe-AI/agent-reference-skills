## Notes

- Roles and permissions of the `serviceusage` service from the IAM roles and permissions reference (retrieved 2026-11-14); role names, descriptions and permission lists are verbatim, regrouped per role. The `service.resource.*` group headings of the official page are omitted from the per-role lists and counts, because they are collapsible headings, not permissions.
- The permissions table lists role IDs only (role names and links are on the official page) and gives service agent roles as a count; service agent roles must not be granted to principals other than service agents.
