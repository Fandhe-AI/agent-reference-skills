# Service Consumer Management IAM roles and permissions

## Signature / Usage

Predefined roles of `serviceconsumermanagement` (role ID, description, included permissions). The permissions table below lists the roles that contain each permission.

### Roles

### Service Consumer Management Admin (`roles/serviceconsumermanagement.admin`)

- Launch stage: Beta
- Admin role for Service Consumer Management
- Permissions (11): `resourcemanager.projects.get`, `resourcemanager.projects.list`, `serviceconsumermanagement.*`, `serviceconsumermanagement.consumers.get`, `serviceconsumermanagement.quota.get`, `serviceconsumermanagement.quota.update`, `serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyu.addResource`, `serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyu.create`, `serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyu.delete`, `serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyu.list`, `serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyu.removeResource`

### Service Consumer Management Viewer (`roles/serviceconsumermanagement.viewer`)

- Launch stage: Beta
- Viewer role for Service Consumer Management
- Permissions (5): `resourcemanager.projects.get`, `resourcemanager.projects.list`, `serviceconsumermanagement.consumers.get`, `serviceconsumermanagement.quota.get`, `serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyu.list`

### Admin of Tenancy Units (`roles/serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyUnitsAdmin`)

- Launch stage: Beta
- Administrate tenancy units
- Permissions (6): `serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyu.*`, `serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyu.addResource`, `serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyu.create`, `serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyu.delete`, `serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyu.list`, `serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyu.removeResource`

### Viewer of Tenancy Units (`roles/serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyUnitsViewer`)

- Launch stage: Beta
- View tenancy units
- Permissions (1): `serviceconsumermanagement.tenancyu.list`
