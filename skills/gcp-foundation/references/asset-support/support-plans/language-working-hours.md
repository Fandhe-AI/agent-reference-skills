# Language Support and Working Hours

Customer Care languages, availability by tier and case priority, Hours of Operation, Business Days, and regional holidays.

## Signature / Usage

Hours of Operation (English and French):

```
5:00 PM on Sunday to 5:00 PM on Friday, Pacific Time Zone
```

Business Days (regional):

```
Japanese:         9:00am-5:00 PM, Monday-Friday, Japan Standard Time (GMT+9)
Mandarin Chinese: 9:00am-5:00 PM, Monday-Friday, China Standard Time (GMT+8)
Korean:           9:00am-5:00 PM, Monday-Friday, Korea Standard Time (GMT+9)
```

## Options / Props

Languages: English, Japanese, Mandarin Chinese, Korean, French. The language of the case determines the language of support.

| Language | Basic | Standard | Enhanced / Premium |
|----------|-------|----------|--------------------|
| English | Billing inquiries only | P2-P4 during Hours of Operation | P1 and P2 24/7 (including holidays); P3 and P4 during Hours of Operation; Chrome browser support 24/7 |
| Japanese | Billing inquiries only | English technical support per English row | P1 and P2 24/7; P3 and P4 during Business Days; Chrome browser support during Hours of Operation |
| Mandarin Chinese | Billing inquiries only | English per English row | P1 and P2 24/7; P3 and P4 during Business Days |
| Korean | Billing inquiries only | English per English row | P1 and P2 24/7; P3 and P4 during Business Days |
| French | Billing inquiries only | English per English row | P1 and P2 24/7; P3 and P4 during Hours of Operation |

Availability depends on case language, support tier, and case priority (P1-P4).

## Notes

- Billing inquiries are supported on all tiers in the listed languages during Hours of Operation or Business Days.
- Customer Care is unavailable on regional holidays unless the case qualifies for 24/7 support. The official page lists the 2026 holiday calendar for The Americas, EMEA, Asia Pacific, Japanese, Korean, and Mandarin Chinese teams (for example 01 January, 25 December for most regions).
- Looker (Google Cloud core) and Apigee technical support is not available in Mandarin Chinese, Korean, and French (English only). SecOps services are English only. Community support and issue trackers are English only.
