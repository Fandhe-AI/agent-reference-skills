# Market Selection

Choosing which of 200+ countries/regions an MSIX app or add-on is offered in, as part of the Pricing and availability page.

## Signature / Usage

On the **Pricing and availability** page, in the **Markets** section, select **Show options** to open the **Market selection** popup. By default all markets are selected; unselect individual markets, or **Unselect all** and add specific ones. A dropdown can filter to **Xbox markets** only.

## Notes

- Market selection applies only to new acquisitions; removing a market later does not revoke access for existing customers in that market, but they will not receive future updates.
- A checkbox controls whether the app is auto-offered in markets the Store adds in the future, using the base price and general availability date.
- Even with all markets selected, local laws, age-rating requirements, or company account verification status can prevent listing in specific countries/regions.
- Certain countries/regions and payment methods (for example, gift card purchases) incur a Commerce Expansion Adjustment deducted from Net Receipts — see the App Developer Agreement for the current schedule.
