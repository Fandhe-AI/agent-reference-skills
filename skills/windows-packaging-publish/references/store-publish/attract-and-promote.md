# Attract-and-Promote Marketing Features

Partner Center tools for driving discovery and conversions after an app is published: promotional codes, custom app-promotion campaigns (CIDs), and sale pricing.

## Signature / Usage

Configured from the app's page in Partner Center: promotional codes and custom promotion campaigns have their own dashboard sections; sales are configured as part of a new submission's Pricing and availability step.

## Options / Props

| Feature | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Promotional codes | Free single-use or multi-use redemption codes for an app or add-on | Configurable activation/expiration window; usable for beta testing, support, or migrating legacy desktop users; app must be fully published first |
| Custom app promotion campaign | Appends a unique campaign ID (CID) to the app's Store URL | Tracks page views/conversions per marketing channel; data surfaces in the Acquisitions report; CID retrievable programmatically from app code |
| Sales (apps and add-ons) | Time-limited reduced price via a lower price tier or percentage discount | Configured in Pricing and availability; requires a new submission to edit or cancel; shown with strikethrough pricing in the Store |

## Notes

- Promotional-code redemption requires the app to already be published to the Store.
- Sale settings live inside the normal submission flow rather than a separate promotions page — changing or canceling a sale means submitting again.
- Campaign/promotion effectiveness is measured through the existing Acquisitions report, not a dedicated marketing-analytics report.
